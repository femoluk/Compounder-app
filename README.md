# ∑ Compounder

**Free. Zero-install. See exactly what a fixed daily % does to your trading account — day by day, compounded.**

A free planning tool from **GivoTrades**. Enter a starting balance, a daily target %, and a date range, and Compounder projects the day-by-day compounded growth of your account. Optionally connect your MT5 account (read-only) and it auto-fills an *Achieved* column from your real daily results.

## Download

➡️ **[Download the latest release](../../releases/latest)** — unzip anywhere, double-click `START_COMPOUNDER.bat`, and your browser opens the app at `http://127.0.0.1:8630`.

No installation. No signup. Runs 100% locally on your own PC — your MT5 credentials never leave your machine.

## What it does

- 📈 Projects compounded growth of a fixed **daily target %** across any date range (Mon–Fri, or include weekends for crypto)
- 📅 Full **day-by-day table**: date, start balance, target profit, end balance, cumulative growth, and an **Achieved Y/N** column
- 🔌 Optional **MT5 connect** — pulls your REAL daily profit/loss and auto-marks each past day Achieved ✓ or ✗ vs the target
- ⬇️ **Export to CSV** for your own spreadsheet
- 📊 Growth curve chart

## Requirements

Windows. That's it — a Python runtime is bundled inside the download.

---

Projections are mathematical illustrations of compounding, **not predictions**. Hitting a fixed daily % every single day is extremely hard. Nothing here is financial advice. Trading carries risk.
